A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
press record.

Does anything get uploaded?

Voice Coach keeps recordings, analysis, and transcripts on your Mac. Acoustic analysis and supported transcription happen on device. Optional transcription models need a one-time download; that doesn’t upload your recordings. You choose if you export or share a file.

Do I need an account?

No Voice Coach account or signup is required. Download the Mac app, open it, and start with a take.

What does it actually measure?

Acoustic signals including pauses, pitch range, loudness, phrase-end energy, and recording quality. When pauses or pitch stand out, Insights can suggest one next practice step. There’s no overall confidence, personality, or audience-reaction score.

Is this an AI speech coach?

It’s a private practice studio. The core Insights shown here use rules based on acoustic measurements. Speech recognition can create an on-device transcript, but the practice loop doesn’t require sending your voice to a cloud AI service.

What is Mimic for?

Mimic lets you practice with a reference: listen, imitate, compare, and retry. You can explore timing, pitch, and emphasis. It doesn’t grade your accent or provide pronunciation scoring.

Is it medical advice or speech therapy?

No. Voice Coach is for acoustic practice. It doesn’t diagnose voice conditions or replace a clinician, speech therapist, or human coach.

Inside the Mac app

Recordings, acoustic analysis, and available transcripts are stored locally under ~/Library/Application Support/VoiceCoach/. The app doesn’t upload your recordings for analysis or transcription, and you don’t need a Voice Coach account.

Permissions & downloads

Recording needs microphone permission. Capturing a Mimic reference from Mac audio needs system-audio permission. Optional Parakeet transcription requires a model/runtime download of about 714 MB. Downloading those files does not send your recordings away.

Exporting & deleting

You choose whether to export audio or reports, and where they go. Deleting a recording removes its audio and analysis after the library update succeeds. Deleting a Mimic removes its reference and attempts. Files you already exported stay wherever you saved them.

This website

This landing page has no analytics scripts, forms, or microphone access. Its fonts, images, and video are served with the site. Sites hosting may use its own access controls and operational logs. Download and feedback links take you to GitHub, which has its own privacy practices.
