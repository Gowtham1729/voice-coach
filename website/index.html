BEFORE YOUR FIRST TAKE

A few things
to know.

Ichido is the new name for Voice Coach. Same local library, with a clearer home for speaking and language practice.

Can I practise in another language?

Choose your speech language in Settings → Transcription. Apple transcribes supported languages on-device; missing models can be downloaded there. Sessions keep their language, and you can re-transcribe an existing take or reference. Optional Parakeet automatically recognises 25 European languages; Japanese requires Apple transcription. Availability varies by language, device, and model.

Will Ichido correct my pronunciation?

Ichido compares acoustic delivery: timing, pitch, and loudness. It does not provide a phoneme-level pronunciation score or a fluency assessment. Reliable matching and similar wording are needed for word-level comparison targets. Listen to the recordings alongside the measured evidence.

What does Words do?

Words is the experimental language inspector, formerly Ask. Enable it in Settings → Experiments to explore transcript meanings, grammar, synonyms, and translations. Chat requires available Apple Intelligence and is off by default. Its answers can be incorrect; it cannot hear or evaluate audio. Conversations are temporary and clear when you quit.

Does anything get uploaded?

Ichido processes recordings, analysis, supported transcription, and language chat on your Mac. It has no recording upload path and requires no account. Update checks and optional speech or translation model downloads use the network. If you explicitly copy an analysis prompt into an external AI service, that service receives what you paste.

What happened to Voice Coach?

Voice Coach is becoming Ichido. The existing app identifier and storage location are preserved, so your local recordings and preferences remain available. The Practice sidebar contains reference sessions previously called Mimics.

What does the name mean?

Say it “ee-chee-doh.” Ichido means “one time” in Japanese. The practice story is inspired by mō ichido, “once more”: listen, give it a take, and try again.

What do I need to run it?

macOS 26 or later. Apple Silicon is recommended, and experimental chat needs available Apple Intelligence. Audio analysis still works when transcription is unavailable. Ichido is free while in early access. It is a practice tool, with acoustic coaching signals; it is not speech therapy or a medical assessment.

What should I know before installing?

The early-access app is ad-hoc signed and not Apple-notarized yet, so macOS may block its first launch. The download details explain permissions and installation. The existing updater feed is retained for Voice Coach users.
