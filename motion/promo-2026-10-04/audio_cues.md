Direction: intimate, curious, lightly rhythmic. Soft percussion, plucked/keyed notes and a short recurring motif. Audition around 100–110 BPM, then measure the actual chosen track. Avoid a victory fanfare, dense pads, lyrics and constant whooshes. Sound should follow real interaction changes rather than every text reveal.

Prefer separate VO/music/SFX stems and 48 kHz WAV masters if the chosen tools support them. Listen on laptop speakers and headphones; duck music under speech and inspect peaks/clipping.
