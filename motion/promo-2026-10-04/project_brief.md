# Project brief

Create one product promo for Ichido, the native Mac speaking-practice app, with launch appeal and a legible app walkthrough. **55 seconds, with a hard 45–60 second range**, landscape 1080p at 60 fps. The viewer should understand private speaking practice for Mac by 9 seconds, see the practice/compare/retry loop, and leave with one action: **Get Ichido for Mac**.

The subject is Ichido and what people can do in the app. The original placement is the existing site video player; placement does not define the film's story.

The central idea is **One more try.** Reference A supplies compact, continuous component motion and purposeful pointer actions. Reference B supplies semantic type reveals, brief scene changes and a decisive closing. Use Ichido's existing ribbon as the brand thread. A new mascot or repeated atmospheric illustration would take time away from the app.

The core demonstration is reference practice. An ordinary recording gets one five-second aside so the viewer knows they can practise with their own voice, too. Words appears once, as the website's optional Understand step inside the French session (added at the user's request in revision 3); settings are omitted. Record/import/capture options appear with the relevant real controls; they do not need separate chapters.

## Scope and responsibilities

This kit contains research, source evidence, selected assets, exact crops, interaction overlays, type/color decisions, script and a production handoff. Later Opus work will make style frames, an animatic and Remotion motion. The user will later choose/generate BGM and VO. The Desktop recording and two Twitter clips supply context/style only.

## Acceptance criteria

- The opening establishes one idea; the app appears by 3 seconds.
- Each explained control has a clear pointer target, a verified response or the documented hover/hold fallback, and time to read.
- Product UI keeps its real names, transcript, chart geometry and values.
- Two practice targets remain conditional on enough words reliably matching.
- Distinct source sessions are separate examples until a consistent fresh capture set replaces them.
- The complete privacy statement is immediately followed by the network exception.
- The closing action holds at least three seconds; total duration stays inside 45–60 seconds.

The video should communicate silently through type and component states, with VO adding explanation. No conversion, learning-outcome or audience-testing claim is implied.
