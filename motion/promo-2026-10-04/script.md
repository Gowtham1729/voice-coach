## Editorial rules

Use a natural, clear read; Ichido is approximately **ee-chee-doh**. The draft is 97 spoken words, leaving space for actions. Do a timed read before motion. If a dense hold needs another second, take it from an intro/transition while keeping the whole film within 60 seconds. Do not speed through the privacy line to fit a music beat.

Type arrives in meaningful phrases, not one bouncing word at a time. One active pointer and one main reading task per shot. In Compare, the app tab label is **Emphasis**; outside the UI, explain it as loudness. Keep Reference/You legend styles unchanged. Another take is an invitation, not evidence of improvement.

The preferred production uses one owned reference through practice, comparison and retry. Current French practice and English comparison are separate examples; introduce the comparison as a new example if those sources remain. The ordinary-recording shot is always a separate path. Exact response-state gaps and usable fallbacks are in `capture_plan.md` and `interaction_plan.json`.

**Understand** follows the website's Listen → Understand → Repeat → Compare sequence. It is film typography, not an app control; the app's own label is **Words**. Words stays optional language help about the transcript: keep "off by default" and "Chat requires available Apple Intelligence" on screen, and keep the panel's own "AI can be wrong. It can't hear or evaluate your audio." visible. Never present it as hearing, judging or coaching the voice.

Settings, detailed engine explanations, a library tour, a separate Speak Along demonstration and extra image interludes have been cut. The brand descriptor is stated once; the ending returns to the opening phrase as a deliberate bookend.
