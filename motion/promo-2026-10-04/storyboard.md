Maintain a screen-space anchor through each component expansion: its center or the clicked control gives the next panel an origin. Interface values never morph into new values to make the transition attractive. A source change between French practice and English comparison is an explicit new example, not an apparently continuous recorded session.

Use a full app window briefly to establish the native Mac product. Most teaching frames are component crops. Decorative ribbon artwork can appear at the bookends, at most one generated image; no room/close-up/night interludes. Typography carries the editorial transitions.
