## Component composition

Briefly establish the full native window, then isolate the exact control or panel being explained. The crop atlas contains eleven unresized, unretouched pieces with source coordinates. Give a selected component room, a restrained shadow and a clear anchor; let it expand or shift into the next verified state. Avoid large simultaneous card mosaics.

At 1080p start with 96 px side margins and 72 px top margin; reserve about 144 px at the bottom if open captions are used. Fit explanations outside the active UI. A target panel needs a generous readable area, not a thumbnail. Keep body text at a source scale that remains legible in the actual website player.

## Pointer and selection

One pointer leads one action. Use `assets/interaction/cursor.svg`, with its documented hotspot. Start around 28 px width; a click ring is optional and subtle. A component focus outline is an editorial annotation. Use it briefly to guide attention, then let the real selected control and next state carry the shot. Click → response → reading hold; no aimless cursor motion.

Faithful editable reconstructions of real controls are allowed for smooth motion. Preserve their actual label, appearance and behavior from source code/captures. Keep data plots, transcript and measured targets as exact captured layers unless a faithful data-backed recreation can be verified. No invented loading, playback, recording, menu or success state. A missing response uses the documented hover/hold fallback.
