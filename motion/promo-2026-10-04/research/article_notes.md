# Adapting the linked article

Source: Movez, [How to build motion design studio with Opus 5.5](https://x.com/0xMovez/article/2104216919033192746), read in the in-app browser on 4 October 2026. Search/web extraction failed, but the browser initially exposed the article body. Embedded demos were not playable there; their smoothness and the author's performance/cost claims were not independently verified.

Useful principles: collect real brand assets before animating; use explicit shot states; make rendering deterministic; define type and motion consistently; plan sound early; review actual rendered frames and playback; recompose layouts for different formats.

This kit turns those principles into product facts, a style guide, a timed script, asset inventory, storyboard, motion specification, audio cues and a review log. The later agent should inspect the images, build representative stills, make an animatic, then iterate.

Adaptations for Ichido: keep the user's installed Remotion project, use the rough film only for context, preserve real UI and measurements, omit unsupported performance statistics, and reserve final music/VO for the user's chosen workflow. Plan scratch audio before fine animation, then revise to the final stems. An 8/10 self-rating is a heuristic, not proof of quality. Human review still matters.

The article's custom seek renderer, synthesis path, API keys, installs and overnight-autonomy suggestions are not needed here. No commands or third-party prompts from the article were executed.
