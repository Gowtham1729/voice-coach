## A: interaction mechanics

| Approximate time | Observed progression | Useful principle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–1.5 | Generate button → loader → check → compact media pill | Keep a stable component origin through a state change |
| 2–5.5 | Media player expands; pointer uses playback/progress/volume controls | An action has a target and visible response |
| 6–7.5 | Switch stretches into Day/Week/Month tabs; pointer selects tabs | A selected shape can travel continuously between choices |
| 7.5–10 | Tabs lead into a chart card; pointer hovers points and changes views | Hold actual data long enough to explain the comparison |
| 10–14 | Command palette, typed search, selection, compact pill, return to button | A component can carry the transition and close the loop |

Its clean stage, low card count, restrained shadows and small purposeful pointer fit Ichido. Borrow continuity and response timing, rather than its black theme, labels, music, album art or example metrics. Arbitrary Generate → music → chart morphs would not explain Ichido; use the app's actual related states.

## The blend for Ichido

**A supplies the interaction; B supplies the sentence.** Say the action as the pointer reaches its real control, show the verified state, then let the viewer read it. Keep one persistent panel anchor through expansion/cropping. Use paper for the product story, cobalt for privacy and paper for the final CTA.

Most of the runtime belongs to product components. The ribbon is a brief brand cue. Select Pitch/Timing/Emphasis as the delivery comparison is explained; focus the real two targets before Try again; select a word in the separate ordinary-take example. `interaction_plan.json` identifies which response states exist and which still need capture. A missing response becomes a truthful focus/hold, not a fabricated result.

60 fps is a proposed delivery choice matching both references. It does not itself guarantee smooth motion; the later production still needs consecutive-frame and playback review.
